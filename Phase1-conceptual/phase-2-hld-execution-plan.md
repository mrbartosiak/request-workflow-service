## Agents and assignments

Use two independent executors and two dedicated reviewers. Each executor receives the same brief and drafts a complete candidate HLD in a separate working directory. Each reviewer is separate from their assigned executor. The coordinating agent manages dependencies and owner approvals.

| Agent | Model | Assignment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executor 1 | GPT‑6.2 Sol, `xhigh` | Draft the full candidate independently. Compare the reviewed candidates for the owner, then assemble the final package from reviewed material and apply the recorded decisions. |
| Reviewer 1 | GPT‑6.2 Astra, `xhigh` | Review every Executor 1 output, including the comparison for the owner, subsequent edits, and the assembled HLD. Delegate corrections directly to Executor 1. |
| Executor 2 | GPT‑6.2 Sol, `xhigh` | Draft the same full scope independently in a separate directory. Revise its candidate as directed by Reviewer 2. |
| Reviewer 2 | GPT‑6.2 Astra, `xhigh` | Review every Executor 2 output and delegate corrections directly to Executor 2. Check the final package across documents for requirement coverage, source fidelity, and consistent application of the owner's decisions. Delegate final-package corrections directly to Executor 1. |

`xhigh` is the proposed setting for “very high.” Confirm both model and reasoning settings when launching each agent. Escalate a required substitution to the owner before launching that assignment. Run the two drafts concurrently. Stagger the two review and revision cycles as needed to stay within four simultaneous agents including the coordinator. Give every agent its complete handoff in the launch prompt so it can work from the workspace and cited sources.

## Review and revision

1. Each executor submits a candidate, its source and decision links, the proposed alternatives, and a list of changed files. Each reviewer receives the shared brief, their executor's candidate, and the review criteria. They check source and lifecycle fidelity, requirement coverage, state and failure semantics, maintainability, status accuracy, and whether modules can be included independently. Compare candidates after each completes its independent review.
2. A reviewer sends each finding directly to the responsible executor as a revision task with the affected file, governing requirement or rationale, and the expected correction. The executor returns the revised files and a response to each finding. The reviewer rechecks the exact revision and records the disposition. Repeat until the reviewer signs off or identifies a decision that needs the owner.
3. Reviewer 1 signs off on the comparison before it goes to the owner. After the decisions are recorded, Executor 1 assembles the final package from reviewed material. Both reviewers check the exact final file revisions, including the index, links, diagrams, and decision log. After an edit, Reviewer 1 rechecks the affected files and Reviewer 2 rechecks any affected cross-document findings and signs off on the final package. Record the reviewer, file revision, and disposition for every executor deliverable, including follow-up work.

## Owner approvals

**Before launching agents.** Approve two independent candidates, two dedicated reviewers, `xhigh` for both models, the repository-root `Phase2-HLD/` location and the AI-edition scope. The owner authorized these choices for this run.

**Before adopting a material design choice.** Present the review-checked alternatives, affected requirements and decision IDs, trade-offs, recommendation, and consequences of deferring. Ask for explicit approval of the affected choice. Cover architecture and extension structure (DEC-10), identity and approvals (DEC-04 and DEC-12), retention and user-visible contracts (DEC-05, DEC-06, DEC-11, and DEC-13), retry and consistency semantics (DEC-08), database or vendor commitments if reopened (DEC-03 and DEC-09), and performance and operating policies (DEC-07 and DEC-14). Apply the same gate to new material changes to scope, delivery guarantees, or risk. Record the owner's choice, rationale, date, and affected documents. Mark unapproved options as proposed or deferred and continue work whose conclusions do not depend on them. [Decision register](decisions.md)

**Before handing off to low-level design.** Present the reviewed HLD index and any unresolved decisions with their downstream dependencies. Ask the owner to approve the handoff and explicitly accept any deferrals that affect it.
