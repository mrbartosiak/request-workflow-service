**Quick check.** Use a runnable Java 25/Spring 7.0.11 build on two instances sharing real PostgreSQL in the proof environment. Keep authentication, current authority, publication witness, disclosure and logging in path. Seed synthetic grants, ≥1,000 eligible browse rows and hidden matches. Prepare distinct eligible targets, keys and observations outside the timed mix. Disable live vendor calls. Warm for 10 s and measure 30 s at 100 independently paced core requests/s. Balance instances and cycle Create, Edit, Verify, Accept, Reject, Delete, Publish and Browse, for 3,000 offers and 375 per operation. Measure from scheduled arrival to full response or timeout, including queueing. Include unstarted and missing requests in the denominator. Verify sampled durable outcomes.

A valid quick run gives a timing signal for the measured workload only. Its profile uses prepared keys and observations, no hot targets and a small data set. Report version, backend, clocks, fixture size, resources, warm-up, split by instance, and eligible successes, conflicts, unexpected statuses, missing/timeouts, max and p50/p95/p99 plus ≤500 ms count and share for each operation. Include total offered/completed count and sampled durable results. Attach sanitized raw timing and logs. Report observed results when an executable environment exists.

**Required acceptance remains** 100 sustained requests per second across at least two service instances. Every measured request completes strictly under 3 s, and ≥95% of each core operation completes within 500 ms under the agreed workload. A timeout, missing response or ≥3 s value fails the per-request threshold. Verify valid throughput at offered load as well as latency. Approve the full data distribution, action/key prep mix, skew, warm-up/duration, resources and response/error denominator before acceptance. Pace open-loop and report dependency impairment, retries, contention or delivery load separately from a normal-load baseline. Keep performance execution out of CI and routine builds.
