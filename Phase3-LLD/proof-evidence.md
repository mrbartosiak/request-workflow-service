**Scope** Q-02/03/09/10/13, R-01/08/10, DEC-04/08/13/14. Deterministic extensions to the two-process PostgreSQL fixture. **Status** Planned, unrun. Exercises the approved proof contract. [Harness](postgres-evidence.md) · [Resolver](proof-resolution.md)

| Cut or interleaving | Expected observations |
| --- | --- |
| Drop the scope prepare response before local effects. Run once with the independent prepare already persisted, then delay the prepare until after proven rollback closes the attempt ID. | No local business effect. Persisted sequence becomes ABORTED on terminal proof. A delayed prepare returns the closed decision without allocating/reopening. Retransmit same ID/digest. A conflicting repeat fails. With rollback proof withheld, the gap stays fenced and the public result reveals no receipt. |
| After known local commit holding both witnesses, withhold one or both confirmations. Confirm publication first in one run and scope first in another. Replay through the other process and exercise authorized browse, history and claim. | One durable ID, transition, receipt and obligation. Before both required confirmations and current scope proof, no protected Publish output, replay success or newly armed send. With public scope closed, privileged proof resolution can still inspect matching evidence. After both confirmations, exact replay uses original ID, and one obligation becomes eligible. Count allowable journal/projection/preparation writes separately. Zero second reservations, receipts, transitions, obligations or business mutations. |
| Remove certified-lineage proof or change the witness's ref/ID, scope, attempt or digest. Restore an older backup. | Resolver rejects mismatches or defers. No confirmation from restored absence. No protected release, repeat effect or new dispatch in affected scope. Compare ordinary scope deferral across missing or concealed receipts. |
| Cut after durable release preparation and before enqueue. | Local commit proved, release prepared and handoff unknown. Verify zero client-confirmed outcomes and zero addition to lost-acknowledged counts. No fabricated handoff. |
| Cut after enqueue, retain trusted handoff evidence, withhold client confirmation. Repeat with handoff evidence missing. | With trusted evidence classify handoff proved/client receipt unknown. Without it classify prepared/handoff unknown. Neither branch counts as lost acknowledged. |
| Confirm independently that the client received the correlated committed result, then restore without that effect. | Classify as positively acknowledged and report its loss separately if trusted recovery cannot reconstruct the effect. Cut before client confirmation to check that queue/network uncertainty does not inflate this count. |
