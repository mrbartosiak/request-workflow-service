# Deferrals 1–4 · Architecture, access and vendor authority

The owner accepted the seven deferrals as Phase 4 constraints. The accountable approver for each disposition is the project owner. The named lead prepares the evidence and recommends scope. Gate receipts identify exact choice, evidence, affected scope, operating roles, conditions, date and approving owner. Documented or synthetic evidence supports a request for bounded target qualification. G2–G5 dispositions name any permitted host, tenant, credentials, data and operations before real contact. B51–B54 then collect target evidence before production promotion under G11. A pending gate leaves dependent actions queued and allows scoped synthetic work under G0 and its local subgates.
