## Confirmed publication

- The synthetic effective-access profile enables Publish with certified scope, independent context and execution grants, an exact observation, and the B16 namespace gate. The owned command transaction assigns the ID and stores state, revision, fact, receipt, publication witness and any applicable scope witness. The shared namespace orders successful local commits across request types and organizations.
- The private publication resolver uses the independent original-lineage journal. Its terminal role is scoped to the organization and decision. Successful decisions leave an audit record. The guarded workflow projection can be completed after the independent decision. Repeat resolution preserves the original allocation and writes no additional business effect.
- `CurrentAccess.permittedPublicationId(actor, guard, ref)` is the field access boundary for B19–B21. It combines current row and independently granted field access with exact guarded publication proof. `CurrentAccess.confirmedPublication(guard, ref)` is the internal association prerequisite for B18 and the publication-dependent dispatch check for B23. B23 must also apply its own claim, cancellation and send grants. If current scope proof is unready the access check defers. With current scope and unresolved publication proof, a matching authorized receipt gives 503 `outcome_unknown`.
