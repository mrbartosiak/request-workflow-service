## First registration and ordering

The command locks the scoped ACCEPTED request before the global namespace. It binds the full attempt to that transaction with a domain-separated, length-prefixed SHA-256 digest. The digest covers namespace, attempt UUID, writer UUID and generation, organization, type, ref, PID, full XID, receipt identity and result revision. Eight transaction-scoped locks encode all 256 bits in eight indexed positions. Their key classes are disjoint from the namespace and migration locks. The one-attempt-per-command rule and the global lock serialize legitimate holders. Matching one word in a particular position cannot complete another binding. The independent observer verifies all eight positions, the database, the namespace lock and the in-progress XID on the same certified backend using one materialized lock snapshot. Registration and the first allocation both verify this binding. This relies on the trusted workflow-command role and one certified live postmaster specified by the fixture.
