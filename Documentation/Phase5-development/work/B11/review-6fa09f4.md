The SQL schema permits ABORTED for both registered and numbered attempts. The verifier treats a numbered ABORTED as valid when its confirmation is absent. An unnumbered ABORTED drops out of the unresolved count. The resolver's ABORT retry also accepts a stored ABORTED without establishing who closed it.
