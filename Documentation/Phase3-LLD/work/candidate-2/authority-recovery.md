# Authority continuity after restore

**Scope** A-05, Q-10/13, R-08/10, DEC-04/05/08/12/14. **Status** Proposed independent authority continuity contract. [Access ordering](access.md) · [Recovery](recovery.md)

`ScopeAuthorityJournal` stores a verified first snapshot and contiguous per-scope ordered change records outside the workflow restore boundary. Cover effective grants and revocations, stable issuer/principal/person bindings, role/group versions, assignments/ownership, type/field policy, trust/freshness stamps, consequential approval/vendor facts, holds/redactions and any request change that alters access or masks. Protect these records as sensitive data. The journal uses the same authority-controlled scope and writer generation as the database. Global policy and identity changes fence every affected scope. External authorities also need current proof under their agreed effective-point contract. [Access](access.md) · [Observations](observations.md)

Authority mutations take the exclusive scope gate. Before any local effect, independently append a numbered PENDING entry with scope, generation, applicable versions and the exact protected delta or a durable pointer and digest. In the same database transaction as the permission-affecting change, commit its scoped authority witness. Local commit remains the effective point for locally ordered grants. An authority mutator returns a definitive success only after the outside journal records the matching committed witness. Track unresolved journal entries, with no skipping sequence gaps. An acknowledged live rollback or equal terminal proof closes a gap as ABORTED. Never use absence in a rewound database to infer rollback. State-changing or retention-triggered visibility effects that cannot be precisely restored must keep the affected row/scope closed. [Transactions](transactions.md) · [Publication proof](publication-reconciliation.md)

A Create may establish visibility for its own new row under the current policy. It cannot modify existing authority inputs or visibility elsewhere. Its provisional ref and row-local originator/assignment stay in the business transaction. Any change to that row's authority baseline follows as a separate transaction after committed Create; no external journal references a provisional ref. [Create reference](identifiers.md)

1. Hard-fence the original writer and protected egress. Obtain an independently attested high-water for each affected scope. Verify the contiguous committed/aborted prefix and resolve PENDING entries with trustworthy original-lineage evidence. A trusted complete durable log plus verified state reconstruction is an alternative proof. Where required, rebuild from authoritative sources that include locally effective assignments and facts, or keep the affected scope closed.
2. Replay committed authority deltas from a verified checkpoint, or install the proved current state and validate versions, digests, targets, redactions and freshness. Restoration must never open older grants when a post-backup revocation may exist. Install the new generation and mark the scope ready only after the restored authority and current external facts pass the check.
3. While proof is unavailable, the gate defers protected reads, context, receipt replay, new effects and dispatch authorization. Prevent success metadata or receipt clues from escaping. Once ready, release decisions, effect authorization and dispatch check the rebuilt effective policy through the regular gate. Remote sends armed before the old fence remain subject to reconciliation. **Drill** Back up a scope where O can browse, Edit and replay and a fixture sender can dispatch on O's behalf. Commit a local revocation and move the assignment after the backup. Restore the image twice. With proof withheld or a journal gap, there are zero protected releases, writes or newly authorized sends. With complete proof installed, O still cannot read or replay, there are zero mutations/sends for O, and a newly assigned authorized actor can act with fresh context. Check all read surfaces, both client and durable outcomes, and ongoing freshness enforcement. [PostgreSQL evidence](postgresql-evidence.md)
