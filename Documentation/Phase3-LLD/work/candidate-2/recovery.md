# Writer generations and recovery safety

**Scope** Q-02/03/10/13, R-01/02/09/10, DEC-03/04/05/08/13/14. **Status** Verified restore within 60 minutes is required. The authority interfaces are proposals. Topology, RPO and operator authority remain open. [HLD recovery](../../../Phase2-HLD/operations-and-recovery.md#database-incident-recovery)

`RecoveryAuthority` orders writer generation, state and database timeline independently of the workflow restore boundary. The independent publication and scope journals, mapping registry and hold/redaction proof survive the same incident. Verify every writer/protected reader uses active proof. Revoke old routing and credentials, terminate and verify old sessions or fence storage access, and prove the old timeline cannot commit after its successor opens. Drain protected readers or fence old response egress. If authority, provenance, high-water or fencing is unproven, keep the affected stage closed. [Publication journal](publication-reconciliation.md) · [Scope continuity](authority-recovery.md)

1. Declare incident and record start. Close writer admission, protected reads with unproven policy, key issuance and remote sends. Secure acknowledgment evidence and possible external effects. Hard-fence prior topology before selecting one trusted timeline.
2. Restore verified backup and trusted retained log when available. Check constraints, histories, versions, independently proved current scope policy and purge state, key evidence and claim/receipt checkpoints. Reconcile the complete publication journal and mappings against committed witnesses; fence gaps. Check binary and migration compatibility.
3. On divergent restore or unproven continuity advance the external generation even when request revisions coincide numerically. New writes and context/cursor tokens require this generation. Known surviving receipts can replay exact intent under current policy and valid key window. Unproven receipts, including absent keys belonging to the prior generation, do not authorize new effects. Held or purged payload in the image is withheld until external ledger checks complete.
4. Reconcile post-recovery-point committed/released evidence by immutable ref/key, generation and commit witness. Use protected independent records captured before possible success release plus any confirmed client acknowledgments. Report known lost acknowledged writes separately from released-but-delivery-unconfirmed and unknown commits. Record incomplete acknowledgment coverage as an evidence gap. No silent reconstruction as new commands.
5. Open verified reads, then fresh commands and publication only after their proofs and authorized checkpoints. Reopen destination workers independently only after remote claims/effect and inbound checkpoint reconciliation; old sends may still apply remotely. Preserve quarantines. Record incident-to-verified-service and separate integration pause times. [Operational controls](operations.md) · [Delivery](delivery.md)

**Alternatives** Trusted backup plus log replay costs less than warm failover and may consume more recovery time. Warm standby can reduce RTO and needs split-brain exclusion and proven commit continuity. Neither path can open if independent proof is unavailable. Recovery constraints preserve identifiers and lifecycle history and must inform the owner decision on permissible loss. [Proposals](proposals.md)
