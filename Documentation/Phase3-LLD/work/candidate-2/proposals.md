# Proposals for the owner comparison

**Scope** A-08 and remaining material choices under DEC-03–14. **Status** Candidate proposals. The kickoff already approves the initial deferrals and delegates bounded defaults for retention, browsing and recovery. [Owner gates](../../owner-approvals.md) · [Kickoff](../../owner-kickoff.md)

| Decision | Recommendation and alternative | Implementation and effect of deferral |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DEC-03/08 Transactions | Explicit transactional locks and atomic state/history/receipt. Compare conditional writes with policy/fact predicates and event-sourced history. Locking is direct to audit and adds waits and deadlock risk. | PostgreSQL evidence can proceed when a backend exists. Production migrations, data adapter and support decisions depend on engine/strategy selection. [Transactions](transactions.md) |
| DEC-04/12 Authority and approvals | Locally ordered grants with an independent continuity journal. Compare verified directory mirror with explicit freshness and effective point. Default unresolved person mappings closed. | Approve provider, person/mapping, field/action matrix, authority proof ownership, operator scope and acceptance. [Access](access.md) · [Restore proof](authority-recovery.md) |
| DEC-05/08 Retention and replay | Signed expiring keys, short receipt window and independent hold/purge proof. Compare UUID/retired markers and protected payload archive. Expiry bounds footprint, adds issuance and clock/signer constraints. | Approve client protocol and proof. Initial 30/180-day body/reason and 7-day receipt proposal is configurable. Holds, archival exposure, backup disposal and remaining terminal policy need explicit decision. [Retention](retention.md) · [Retries](retries.md) |
| DEC-06/08/11 Public wire | Named action routes, distinct action context and exact validated field/cursor semantics. Compare one aggregate transition endpoint, inline context, alternative page caps and snapshot/offset. Named routes clarify action payloads at cost of more operations. | Specify exact types, wire/errors, token/encoding and field grants before compatibility baseline. Delegated browse properties retain default 10 and established filters/order/masks. [REST](rest.md) · [Browse](browse-history.md) |
| DEC-13/08 Identifiers and recovery fence | Independent ref-bound Publish reservation and commit journal, local serialization, global generations and immutable mappings. Compare pre-reserved ranges plus separate complete association proof. Journal calls add dependency and latency. | Approve topology, namespace/key migration, commit proof and fencing. Unproved targets stay closed. [Identifiers](identifiers.md) · [Publication proof](publication-reconciliation.md) |
| DEC-09/10 Delivery and packaging | Compose independent claim/authorize workers, per-tenant proof capabilities and fake conformance in one release. Compare broker relay, polling and larger worker budgets. | Approve module edges, proof classification and worker fixture/budget; live priorities, privacy, linkages, safe privileges and sandbox proofs remain deferred. [Delivery](delivery.md) · [Vendors](vendors.md) |
| Tooling | Pilot CI API diff and test-only boundary rules. Defer DTO/mapping/SQL/event generation pending measurable volume, exact compatibility and proof. | Keep explicit classes/contracts and gain evidence through prototypes. Material dependencies require separate approval. [Tooling](tooling.md) |
| DEC-07 Performance | Short owner-authorized smoke is candidate evidence where feasible. Maintain separately approved acceptance workload. | Approve full load environment, denominators, healthy/fault mixes and traffic shape before formal acceptance. [Acceptance](acceptance.md) |
| DEC-14 Operations | Protected spool, risk-based advisory review and critical branch gates. Compare backup/log restore versus warm standby, subject to 60-minute verified service target. | Approve risk thresholds, exception owners, topology, RPO/loss, proof source, named restore authorities and final rollout gates. Recovery tuning values are configurable within scope. [Operations](operations.md) |

The owner must accept any remaining deferral affecting Phase 4 planning after package signoffs. Required lifecycle, identifiers and acceptance outcomes are binding. [Handoff](../../owner-approvals.md#phase-4-handoff)
