# Restore fencing and verified resumption

**Scope** A-04/07, F-05/09, Q-02/03/09/10/13, R-01/03/09/10, DEC-03/04/05/08/13/14. Verified resumption within 60 minutes from incident declaration is required. Controls and settings are proposed. Topology, RPO, acceptable loss and authority remain open. [HLD](../../../Phase2-HLD/operations-and-recovery.md)

| Stage | Required proof before proceeding |
| --- | --- |
| Declare and contain | Record start and impact. Stop admitting writes, dispatches and stale disclosures. Remove old instance access and physically isolate or demote the previous writer, revoke its write path, and prove outstanding old transactions cannot commit. Close old dispatch claims everywhere. Unproven exclusion blocks activation. The independent authority reserves a fresh never-reused generation after validating the fence. |
| Recover privately | Restore trusted data and authoritative logs. Check schema, request/history, identity, grants, receipts, mappings and inbox/outbox checkpoints. Replay the independent hold/purge ledger and point authoritative traffic at the recovered primary. Preserve historical creation tuples. [Payload recovery](retention.md) |
| Reconcile loss | Compare independent acknowledged-effects evidence, earliest/latest replay coverage and committed log evidence with restored rows. Build protected lists of missing acknowledged writes, unacknowledged ambiguous attempts, publication reservations and external effects, with operator decisions. Detect and report when the full loss count is unknowable. Reconcile current grants, mutable policy/evidence and delayed remote sends before releasing related surfaces. |
| Verify and stage | Prove allocator watermark and prior writer exclusion. Rebuild known published associations and mapping values with the original identities. Reconstruct eligible receipts with original facts from trustworthy evidence. Reopen current authorized reads, then safe command types/scopes, then separately reconciled dispatch, in bounded steps. Record times, criteria, RPO discrepancy, lost acknowledged writes and operator authority. |

`RecoveryFence` stores the active strictly increasing signed-64-bit epoch in the independent authority and recovered database. This is the token generation and new Create epoch. Writers, key issue, release and dispatch prove it is current. Old observations and cursors fail even if revisions repeat. Old keys may return trustworthy retained results under current permissions and retention. Old keys cannot cause new effects. Prove completeness for any scope reopened to new commands. Suspend requests with unknown prior effects. A lost publication link needs original identity recovery or target fencing. [Identifiers](identifiers.md) · [Retry contract](retries.md)

**Evidence sources.** Propose independently protected archived commit history and an append-only minimal acknowledgement witness before success release. Record authorized audit identity, operation digest/ref, revision and ID/obligation identifiers when applicable. A witness can precede a lost response. Distinguish release records from confirmed client observations where known. A commit can occur before its witness append. An absent witness cannot prove absence of commit. No old-key effects or uncertain target actions resume on witness absence alone. Require the authoritative log or keep the affected scope fenced. Retain abort/no-commit evidence, audit and key verification materials outside the restore boundary with separately approved policy. Data access to recovered records stays subject to fresh authorization. [Operating policy](operations.md)

**Comparison and drill.** Backup plus verified log recovery minimizes standing capacity and can elongate the RTO. Warm failover shortens startup and increases capacity, replication and split-brain costs. Recommend rehearsing backup recovery first. Select production topology against measured 60-minute results and approved RPO. Proposed drill milestones are declaration 0, fencing 10 min, usable dataset 35 min, reconciliation and scoped checks 50 min, verified service 60 min. These are configurable drill budgets. Report partial resumption separately from verified service. Exercise old-backup rewind, stale writer rejoin, missing independent proof and ambiguous remote effect. Capture before/after durable facts and evidence of fence/hold enforcement. [Verification](verification.md)
