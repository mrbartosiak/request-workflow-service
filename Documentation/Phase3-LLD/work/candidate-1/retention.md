# Payload retention and holds

**Scope** F-04/06, Q-03/11/13, R-07/08/10, DEC-04/05/11/14. Mandatory reason validation, complete lifecycle facts and restricted deleted payload are required. The compared policies and mechanisms are proposed. Select one payload policy after review. [Owner item 7](../../owner-kickoff.md) · [HLD history](../../../Phase2-HLD/workflow-and-history.md)

| Proposed policy | Access, holds and restore impact |
| --- | --- |
| A. Timed protected retention | Retain an ordinary body while the request is in scope. Propose eligibility to purge deleted body after 30 days and each reason after 90 days from its transition. Initial public responses continue to mask both on deleted requests and reasons everywhere. A separately approved restricted read grant can inspect retained payload until purge. A hold has time to arrive but more sensitive payload remains in scope during that window. |
| B. Terminal minimization | In the terminal transaction, retire the body on Delete and retain only a reason-present fact for Delete/Reject unless a hold is effective. Validate the reason before commit. Commit a retirement marker atomically with each terminal effect and carry it to the independent recovery witness before success release. Rollback leaves no retirement. Historical access is limited to lifecycle facts and held material, with fewer sensitive bytes and less opportunity for later holds. Restore applies proven terminal markers and holds before reopening the scope. |

Recommend A pending owner review of protected access, hold and purge authority, and organizational retention policies. B has fewer retained sensitive values and requires a separately approved change to the reason/body transaction. Both preserve request identity, terminal state, contiguous history, publication association and distinct receipt policy. Safety checks apply whatever periods the owner delegates. [Decision proposal](decisions.md) · [Receipt policy](retries.md)

**Common ordering proposal.** Apply holds, hold release, retention visibility and purge under the exclusive organization guard. A hold effective first blocks retirement. A durably committed retirement fences that payload version, including on cleanup failure or restore. Subsequent holds preserve remaining material and report that version unavailable. For background purge, commit the decision to the independent hold/purge ledger before physical deletion. For B’s immediate terminal path, commit the marker in the terminal transaction before witness recording or cleanup. Finish physical deletion idempotently. If commitment is uncertain, suspend affected payload release and purges pending proof. Read/release uses the guard and effective decisions. Reapply ledger and committed terminal markers before opening a restore to reads. Record actor/authority, payload version, basis, time and verified cleanup, with no payload text. **Evidence.** Exercise hold-before-purge and purge-before-hold using forced guard order, cleanup failure after logical purge and restore of an earlier copy containing the payload. Under both policies, verify an authorized pre-purge view and no exposure after purge, no revived replay, and the same complete ordered terminal facts. Record how many payload versions remain accessible and what can be held under each policy. [E7](verification.md)
