# Logging, release gates and resilience

**Scope** A-07, Q-05/06/07/10, R-01/03/05/07/09/10, DEC-07/09/14. Buffered logs, risk-based advisories, critical-path gates and verified resumption are approved directions. Proposed settings require agreement except values within delegated recovery configuration. [Operating policy](../../../Phase2-HLD/operating-policy-alternatives.md)

**Request/error records.** At ingress and egress log every request and its outcome, including pre-auth rejects and transport errors. Record every vendor send and timeout. Use UTC instant, direction, safe correlation, operation/route template, destination alias, outcome, elapsed time and safe failure stage/error class with catalogued descriptions. Exclude bodies, reasons, names, IDs where not operationally needed, raw URLs/query values, arbitrary exception text, raw keys/tokens, credentials and third-party payloads. An authenticated interface may correlate an incident using restricted lookup. Log accesses and recovery operations separately from workflow history. Use approved encrypted buffering and restrict log access by duty.

Propose an encrypted persistent 128 MiB per-instance spool plus independently budgeted edge reject logging. Reserve capacity for request start/end and error before accepting service or authorizing outbound work. Alert at 70%, restrict admission at 85%, and drain with reserved error/reject headroom and protected edge path. If even reject evidence cannot be preserved, stop routing to the unavailable instance and report Q-05 evidence gaps. Retain protected service logs for proposed 30 days. Test sink outage, drain/reorder, credentials in bad inputs, overload admits/rejects and fault paths. Compare records to observed request and attempt counts. Synchronous sink admission raises latency and outage impact. Best-effort drops undermine completeness. Buffered fail-closed admission protects evidence at the cost of spool sizing and operations. [Priority and review](decisions.md)

**Advisories.** Check a resolved inventory (direct and transitive) weekly and before every release against Spring Security Advisories and Snyk. Log date, sources, versions, findings including none, applicability/exposure and action owner. Propose block when review is missing or an applicable critical/high finding is unresolved, and for any advisory with credible exposure requiring immediate response. Propose mitigation review within 1 business day for blocking issues and target 7 days for eligible medium applicability. Approve exception authority, deadline, compensating controls and expiry before any override. Past-due exceptions block the gate. [Spring advisories](https://spring.io/security/) · [Snyk database](https://security.snyk.io/)

**Quality.** Propose per-method cyclomatic complexity ≤10 in lifecycle, validation, authorization, receipt coordination and delivery state decisions, and ≥90% branch coverage in those paths. Use a Java 25-compatible pinned JaCoCo release with source debug info and corroborate with critical case completion, module-boundary checks and production-engine concurrency evidence. Gate missing fixtures even if coverage reaches threshold. Simpler alternative is reviewed complexity plus manual coverage collection, with inconsistent enforcement risk. Report exceptions, impact, owners and missed required outcomes. [JaCoCo counters](https://www.jacoco.org/jacoco/trunk/doc/counters.html) · [Evidence](verification.md)

**Pressure and recovery.** Record queue depth/age, pool and lock waits, admission/errors, per-destination lag/unknown count, slow or stale authority, log rejection gaps and generation proof state. Bound client and worker retry amplification. Gradually resume after faults and test migration/compatible rollback with existing records, retained receipts, policy, mappings and pending work. Proposed initial wakeup alerts are unknown delivery >0, authority proof lost, and p95 lock wait >100 ms over 5 min. Exercise verified recovery with audited pause/recovery/reconciliation/resume. The target is verified service within 60 min from declaration. Report lost acknowledged writes separately. [Recovery](recovery.md) · [Performance](performance.md)
