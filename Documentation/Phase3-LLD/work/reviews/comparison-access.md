Start with configurable eligibility at 30 days for deleted bodies and 90 for reasons (C2 proposes 180 for reasons). Retain other bodies within their approved record scope. Ordered holds can delay purge and never revive a retired version or extend replay. Reapply independent hold/purge proof before opening restored reads. Initial history requires a distinct grant and withholds actor and reason. Any archive access, other terminal-body policy and hold/purge authority remain open.
