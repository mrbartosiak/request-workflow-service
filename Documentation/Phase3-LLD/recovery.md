# Restore fencing and verified resumption

**Scope** A-04/07, F-05/09, Q-02/03/09/10/13, R-01/03/09/10, DEC-03/04/05/08/13/14. Verified resumption within 60 minutes from incident declaration is required. D6-R selects a backup plus verified log rehearsal first. Production topology, RPO, permitted loss and named authority remain open. [HLD](../Phase2-HLD/operations-and-recovery.md)

| Stage | Required proof before proceeding |
| --- | --- |
| Declare and contain | Record start/impact. Close unsafe writes, claims and protected egress. Physically exclude the old writer and prove outstanding transactions cannot commit. Revoke routing/credentials and drain or fence old response egress. Exclude old dispatch claims locally. Confirm exclusion before certifying a higher epoch. |
| Recover privately | Restore trusted backup/logs. Verify schema/history, grants/facts, receipts, mappings and checkpoints. Apply hold/purge decisions. Preserve original tuples and witnesses. Prove source/tenant/domain inbox coverage before admission. [Current scope](authority-continuity.md) · [Inbox](inbound-recovery.md) |
| Reconcile loss | Compare retained log and independent evidence to restored rows. Report ambiguous commit, proved commit/prepare with handoff unknown, proved handoff/client receipt unknown and positively acknowledged effects separately. Only positively acknowledged missing effects count as lost acknowledged writes. Report incomplete evidence explicitly. Reconcile delayed remote sends. |
| Verify and stage | Prove watermark and original publication associations separately. Recover original effects or fence affected targets, and the namespace on incomplete coverage. Restore mappings and eligible receipts only from trusted evidence. Open currently authorized reads, then safe writes, then reconciled dispatch. Audit time, lost acknowledgments and authority. [Journal](publication-journal.md) |

`RecoveryFence` stores the active strictly increasing signed-64-bit epoch in the independent authority and recovered database. This is the token generation and new Create epoch. Writers, key issue, release and dispatch prove it is current. Old observations and cursors fail even if revisions repeat. Old keys may return trustworthy retained results under current permissions and retention. Old keys cannot cause new effects. Prove completeness for any scope reopened to new commands. Suspend requests with unknown prior effects. A lost publication link needs original identity recovery or target fencing. [Identifiers](identifiers.md) · [Retry contract](retries.md)

**Evidence sources.** Keep trusted commit history outside the restore boundary with approved retention. Before a success handoff append `ReleasePrepared`. Record handoff and client receipt separately when independently demonstrated. A missing witness cannot prove absence of commit, and preparation cannot prove handoff. Internal proof resolution can proceed while scope access stays closed. Reopen only on verified evidence. [Evidence levels](witness-records.md) · [Resolver](proof-resolution.md) · [Cuts](proof-evidence.md)

**Comparison and drill.** Backup plus verified log recovery minimizes standing capacity and can elongate the RTO. Warm failover shortens startup and increases capacity, replication and split-brain costs. Run the backup/log rehearsal first when available. Select production topology against measured 60-minute results and approved RPO. Proposed drill milestones are declaration 0, fencing 10 min, usable dataset 35 min, reconciliation and scoped checks 50 min, verified service 60 min. These are configurable drill budgets. Report partial resumption separately from verified service. Exercise old-backup rewind, stale writer rejoin, missing independent proof and ambiguous remote effect. Capture before/after durable facts and evidence of fence/hold enforcement. [Verification](verification.md)
