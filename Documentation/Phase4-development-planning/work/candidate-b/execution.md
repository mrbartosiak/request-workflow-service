# Phase 5 execution and review

**Entry** Verify the accepted Phase 4 revision, its exact manifest and owner receipt `G0`. Confirm ticket predecessors and gate receipts for the phase of work you are about to start. Staged gates allow drafting or evidence collection before authorizing their dependent run or promotion. Pin the revision and workspace commit in the assignment. Begin with the ticket sources and predecessor handoff artifacts. Use short branches or isolated worktrees with stable ticket IDs. The coordinator assigns shared contract files, schema migrations, and bootstrap wiring to one integrator at a time. ## Ticket cycle

1. An executor records the linked source clause, expected behavior, fixture, commands, scope and prerequisites. Capture the initial test result against the baseline. A new test must fail for the intended assertion or capability. Record unrelated environment/setup failure separately. For fault evidence, use deterministic barriers and a negative control that proves the observation can detect the violation. Remove fault controls from release artifacts.
2. Make the smallest production change that satisfies the red oracle. Refactor with the test green. Exercise the normal entry point and rerun impacted predecessor cases. A verification-only ticket adds an executable scenario and assertions. If correct existing behavior passes, preserve it and prove the oracle catches a fixture perturbation, with the perturbation absent from the delivered change. Build readiness tasks use a reproducible preflight or build check as their first failing assertion.
3. Attach a before/after evidence record with ticket IDs, exact code and plan revisions, gate receipts, fixture and tool versions, sanitized observations, reproduction command, time and pass/fail decision. Include applicable E/P IDs, HTTP body/headers, barrier and wait observations, authoritative before/after counts, negative controls, coverage delta and outstanding gaps. Keep raw evidence access-controlled. [Functional format](../../../Phase3-LLD/verification.md) · [PostgreSQL format](../../../Phase3-LLD/postgres-evidence.md)
4. The reviewer reruns or inspects the evidence and source-to-oracle alignment and records disposition on the exact commit. Review stops merges on failed oracle, missing authority or impact beyond the assignment. Reviewer assigns fixes directly to the owning executor and rechecks the response. The coordinator merges after disposition and verifies downstream assumptions against the resulting revision.
5. Hand off command to reproduce, owned files or contracts, resulting commit, evidence path, residual exposure, linked successor IDs and any new owner decision. Mark unresolved issues blocked at the dependency they affect. The coordinator may continue other ready assignments. [Risks](risks-engineering.md) · [Decisions](choices.md)

Conventions in the plan: each ticket lists direct predecessors under **Needs** and a concise **Stop** criterion and handoff. Implicit dependencies are transitive. All Phase 5 tickets inherit `G0`. Gate IDs use the choices and deferrals modules. Named roles are functions for the owner to staff. One executor and one reviewer own each ticket evidence record. Production actions require their applicable gate even when a local fixture has passed. [Gate map](deferrals-design.md) · [Operating gates](deferrals-operating.md)
