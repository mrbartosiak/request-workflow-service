**Status** Proposals. At one owner checkpoint after synthesis and independent review, present C1–C5 together with the selected plan, exact manifest and recommendation for the G0 first wave. The owner can accept exact selections with conditions or return decisions individually. Record owner identity, date, selected choices and exact manifest.
